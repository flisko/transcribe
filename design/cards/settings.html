Settings — General

Single pane, ~420px, opened by the toolbar gear or ⌘,. Five controls total — every value persists instantly, no Apply button. The Model and Language keys are shared with the main-window quick settings.

Fast (large-v3-turbo)About 4x faster. Slightly less accurate, mainly on smaller languages.
Medium (medium) A good middle ground when the big models feel slow. Half the size of Fast.
Small (small)Much faster and much smaller. Fine for clear speech in major languages.
Base (base)Very fast rough drafts. Expect mistakes, especially outside English.
Tiny (tiny)The fastest possible pass. Only for quick gist checks.
