One-time setup

Transcribe needs a few free components before it can start. Everything runs on your Mac — nothing is uploaded.

Speech recognition (whisper) Installed
Audio converter (ffmpeg) Installed
Language models (4.6 GB download) Not installed
Link downloader (yt-dlp) — optional, only needed for video links Not installed
Run Setup… Check Again

Setup opens the Terminal and takes a few minutes, mostly downloading. It’s safe to run more than once.

• Engine-missing special case swaps the checklist for: “Transcribe can’t find its engine. Keep Transcribe.app inside the Transcribe folder, next to “bin” and “models”, then click Check Again.” • During this state, drop attempts show the overlay with “Finish setup to start transcribing”.
