ETA text rules

  • “Transcribing — 5% · estimating time…”Before the smoothed rate stabilizes — never show a wild first guess.
  • “Transcribing — 37% · about 8 min left”Coarse rounding (“about”), from an exponentially-smoothed percent-per-second rate.
  • “about 1 min left”Minutes only — seconds imply false precision.
  • “less than a minute left”The final stretch; never counts down to zero.
  • Monotone displayThe shown ETA never jumps upward by more than one step between refreshes — a thrashing ETA reads as broken.
